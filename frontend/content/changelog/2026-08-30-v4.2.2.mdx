Release 4.2.2 elevates SRA into a mature, persistent AI workspace. Users can start long-running requirements analyses, leave the application, return later, find their work instantly, understand what each agent produced, continue from where they left off, and trust that their work is durable and recoverable.

## Global Search & ⌘K Command Palette

Quickly navigate the entire workspace with the new universal command palette (`⌘K` / `Ctrl+K`):

- **Debounced Multi-Entity Search**: Instant full-text search across specifications, parent projects, and vector knowledge chunks with strict user isolation.
- **Quick-Action Shortcuts**: Create analyses, inspect active tasks, jump directly to BYOK settings, or initiate specification drafting tailored to specific standards (IEEE 830, ISO 29148, Volere, Agile PRD).

## Global Activity Center & Checkpoint Recovery

Long-running asynchronous AI jobs are now easily monitored and managed from any surface:

- **Global Slide-Over Drawer**: Filter runs across *All*, *Running*, *Completed*, and *Failed* tasks.
- **Live Stage Progress**: Real-time progress bars and descriptive failure reasons.
- **1-Click Checkpoint Resume**: Re-launch interrupted or out-of-quota runs from their exact stage checkpoint without re-paying for completed agents.

## Welcome Back Catch-Up & Notification Center

- **Welcome Back Catch-Up Notifier**: Automatically detects specifications that completed or required attention while away from the browser tab.
- **Header Notification Center**: Persistent popover tracking recent finished jobs with unread badges and local state sync.

## Contextual Next Actions & Task Inspector

- **Next Recommended Actions**: Contextual continuation bar on specification documents providing 1-click Word (.docx) export, React Flow Data Flow Diagram (DFD) generation, CLI traceability sync, chat refinement, and knowledge base finalization.
- **Task & Execution Inspector**: Deep inspection dialog revealing provider/model provenance, 5-layer pipeline execution milestones, and 6Cs rubric quality scores (Completeness, Consistency, Clarity, Correctness, Conformity, Conciseness).
- **Smart Starter Templates & Continue Working Hub**: Pre-populated industry templates (E-Commerce, HIPAA Telehealth, FinTech Ledger, AI Agents) and priority continue working hero cards.
